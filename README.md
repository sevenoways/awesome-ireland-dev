## Open data portals

- [data.gov.ie](https://data.gov.ie/) - National open data portal with thousands of datasets and a CKAN API.
- [Smart Dublin](https://data.smartdublin.ie/) - Open data from the four Dublin local authorities.
- [Cork Open Data](https://data.corkcity.ie/) - Datasets from Cork City Council.

## Statistics

- [CSO PxStat](https://data.cso.ie/) - Central Statistics Office tables with a JSON-stat REST API.
- [CSO Census](https://www.cso.ie/en/census/) - Census results and small-area statistics.

## Transport

- [NTA GTFS feeds](https://www.transportforireland.ie/transitData/PT_Data.html) - Static GTFS timetables for Dublin Bus, Bus Éireann, Luas, Irish Rail and others.
- [NTA Developer Portal](https://developer.nationaltransport.ie/) - GTFS-Realtime vehicle positions and trip updates (free API key).
- [Irish Rail Realtime API](http://api.irishrail.ie/realtime/) - Live train positions, station departures and timetables (XML).
- [Dublin Bikes (JCDecaux)](https://developer.jcdecaux.com/) - Live station availability for dublinbikes.

## Weather and environment

- [Met Éireann Open Data](https://www.met.ie/climate/available-data) - Historical climate data and forecast API.
- [waterlevel.ie](https://waterlevel.ie/) - OPW real-time river and lake levels, with GeoJSON feeds.
- [EPA Ireland Maps & Data](https://gis.epa.ie/) - Environmental Protection Agency GIS data and downloads.
- [Air Quality Ireland](https://airquality.ie/) - Live air quality monitoring from the EPA.

## Maps and geography

- [Tailte Éireann Open Data](https://data-osi.opendata.arcgis.com/) - Boundaries (counties, electoral divisions, townlands) from the former Ordnance Survey Ireland.
- [Townlands.ie](https://www.townlands.ie/) - Crowd-sourced townland boundaries from OpenStreetMap.
- [OpenStreetMap Ireland](https://www.openstreetmap.ie/) - The Irish OSM community.
- [Geofabrik Ireland extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/ireland-and-northern-ireland.html) - Daily OSM data downloads for the island.

## Addresses and postcodes

- [Eircode](https://www.eircode.ie/) - Official postcode system. Address lookup is a licensed (paid) product.
- [GeoDirectory](https://www.geodirectory.ie/) - The official address database (commercial).
- [irish-validators](https://github.com/sevenoways/irish-validators) - Validate and format Eircodes, PPSNs, Irish IBANs and phone numbers.

## Business and company data

- [CRO Open Data](https://opendata.cro.ie/) - Companies Registration Office datasets of registered companies.
- [Revenue tax rates](https://www.revenue.ie/en/vat/vat-rates/index.aspx) - Current VAT rates (no API, but the reference).

## Government and parliament

- [Oireachtas Open Data API](https://api.oireachtas.ie/) - Debates, bills, votes and members of the Dáil and Seanad.
- [Irish Statute Book](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/) - Acts and statutory instruments.
- [gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/) - Central government publications and services.

## Health

- [HSE Open Data](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/publications/) - HSE reports and statistics.
- [HIQA inspection reports](https://www.hiqa.ie/reports-and-publications) - Health and social care inspection reports.

## Libraries

- [irish-validators](https://github.com/sevenoways/irish-validators) - Eircode, PPSN, IBAN and phone validation (TypeScript).
- [ireland-data](https://github.com/sevenoways/ireland-data) - Counties with Irish names and a bank holiday calculator.

## Community

- [Tech Ireland](https://techireland.org/) - Directory of Irish tech companies and investors.
- [r/DevelEire](https://www.reddit.com/r/DevelEire/) - Subreddit for software developers in Ireland.
